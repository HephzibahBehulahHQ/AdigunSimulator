      Simulation Control
      
          
      Available analyses: LED current limiter and unloaded two-resistor voltage divider.
      
          
          
          
      
            
            
      Generic Shockley diodes with optional series resistance and generic Ebers–Moll NPN/PNP BJTs; parameters are not manufacturer-verified.

              Net Classes & Rules
              
      
              Applying membership moves selected nets from other classes. Removing a class removes its pair rules; unassigned nets use board rules. Copper does not move. Undo reverses each applied edit.
              
      
              
        
              
          
          
          
          
          
              Member nets
              
          
              
        
              
        
              
          
          
          
          
          
              The stricter board, class and pair limits apply. A same-class rule checks different nets, never a net against itself. Changed rules can block fabrication until violations are corrected.
              
          
              
        
              
      
              
      
              
      
                  
    
      
              
        
              Board Setup
              
        
              Rectangular one/two-layer board. Dimensions do not scale or move components. Rules are design limits, not manufacturing approval.
