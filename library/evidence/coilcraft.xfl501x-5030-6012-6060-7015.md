## Symbol and PCB mapping

The footprint assets cite each Coilcraft package drawing. Local pad 1 follows the stripe marking the winding start. XFL5015, XFL5018 and XFL5030 use 1.18 × 4.70 mm lands on 3.31 mm centers. XFL6012 and XFL6060 use 1.43 × 5.50 mm lands on 4.04 mm centers. XFL7015 uses 1.78 mm wide lands on 4.72 mm centers. Its land length is 6.22 mm for codes 251, 471 and 681, and 6.16 mm for codes 102 and 152.

The native RL fixtures cover all six packages and both XFL7015 land lengths. The analysis uses ideal inductance. Breadboard placement requires a documented breakout.
