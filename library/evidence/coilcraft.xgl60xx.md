# Coilcraft XGL6030 and XGL6060 ordering evidence

The [XGL6030 ordering table](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/power/shielded-inductors/molded-inductor/xgl/xgl6030/) supplies 21 MEC codes from 0.065 to 18 µH. The [XGL6060 ordering table](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/power/shielded-inductors/molded-inductor/xgl/xgl6060/) supplies 20 MEC codes from 0.22 to 47 µH. Both list ±20% inductance tolerance. Lifecycle, native geometry and model validation remain pending, along with independent review.
