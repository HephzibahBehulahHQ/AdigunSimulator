# Coilcraft XGL6030 and XGL6060 ordering evidence

The [XGL6030 ordering table](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/power/shielded-inductors/molded-inductor/xgl/xgl6030/) supplies 21 MEC codes from 0.065 to 18 µH. The [XGL6060 ordering table](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/power/shielded-inductors/molded-inductor/xgl/xgl6060/) supplies 20 MEC codes from 0.22 to 47 µH. Both list ±20% inductance tolerance. Lifecycle, native geometry and model validation remain pending, along with independent review.

## Symbol and PCB mapping

The [XGL6030 drawing](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/04af5f0e-159c-4c20-b5cf-d118de0d6d2a/xgl6030d.gif?lang=en-US) and [XGL6060 drawing](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/73eea929-3e6a-4a38-af9e-f0d053842c3a/xgl6060d.gif) specify 1.43 × 5.50 mm lands on 4.04 mm centers. The standard-termination maximum heights are 3.10 mm and 6.10 mm respectively. Local pad 1 follows the winding-start stripe.

The RL fixtures cover XGL6030-102MEC and XGL6060-103MEC. They check winding-start routing, PCB DRC, Gerber, BOM, ideal RL scope data and the breadboard breakout diagnostic. Physical breakout placement, part-specific loss and saturation validation, and independent review remain pending.
