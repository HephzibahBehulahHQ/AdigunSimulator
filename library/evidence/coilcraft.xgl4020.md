# Coilcraft XGL4020 ordering evidence

The [Coilcraft XGL4020 datasheet](https://www.coilcraft.com/getmedia/76c9c081-4945-4c85-9129-9356e1ad6734/xgl4020.pdf) and the [manufacturer ordering table](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/power/shielded-inductors/molded-inductor/xgl/xgl4020/) support the 15 MEC candidates from 0.040 to 8.2 µH with ±20% tolerance.
