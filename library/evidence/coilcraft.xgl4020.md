# Coilcraft XGL4020 ordering evidence

The [Coilcraft XGL4020 datasheet](https://www.coilcraft.com/getmedia/76c9c081-4945-4c85-9129-9356e1ad6734/xgl4020.pdf) and the [manufacturer ordering table](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/power/shielded-inductors/molded-inductor/xgl/xgl4020/) support the 15 MEC candidates from 0.040 to 8.2 µH with ±20% tolerance. These use standard termination and 7-inch reel packaging. Lifecycle, native mapping, model validation and independent review remain pending.

## Shared XGL4020 land pattern

The [manufacturer drawing](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/9c9def4a-72ad-4389-8f41-d531b2380a39/xgl4020d.gif?lang=en-US) gives 0.98 × 3.40 mm lands on 2.37 mm centers and a 2.10 mm maximum height for standard termination. Local pad 1 marks the striped winding start. All 15 codes have checksummed symbols and footprints. The native RL fixture covers XGL4020-102MEC and checks the breakout diagnostic, routed PCB, DRC, Gerber, BOM, and ideal RL scope data.
