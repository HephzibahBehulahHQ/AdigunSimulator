# Vishay TSOP38438

Candidate sourced from the [Vishay datasheet](https://www.vishay.com/docs/82491/tsop382.pdf). Pin 1 is active-low OUT, pin 2 is GND and pin 3 is VS. The Minicast body and 2.54 mm lead pitch follow the package drawing. Example 072 supplies the educational drill and pad convention and documents insulated pigtails. No numerical optical model or independent approval is attached.
