# Coilcraft POExxPR ordering evidence

[Coilcraft’s POExxPR specification tables](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/transformers/power-transformers/power-over-ethernet/poexxpr/) list six flyback and six forward-mode surface-mount transformer order codes with the ED suffix. The tables link to schematics and separate the two topologies. Exact pin maps, PCB assets, and simulation validation are pending.
