# LM35DZ/NOPB

See the [manufacturer datasheet and package drawing](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm35.pdf). Pin 1 is +VS, pin 2 is VOUT, and pin 3 is GND. The nominal transfer has 10 mV/°C gain and 0 mV intercept. The staggered educational footprint derives from [Example 064](../../examples/064-arduino-temperature-monitor.adigun.json). Hardware fit, numerical device model, and human review remain open.
