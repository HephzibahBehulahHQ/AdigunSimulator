## PCB and symbol evidence

The XGL3520 and XGL3530 drawings specify 0.81 × 3.20 mm lands on 1.80 mm centers. The XGL4012 and XGL4015 drawings specify 0.98 × 3.40 mm lands on 2.37 mm centers. Each footprint references its drawing and records its own maximum package height. Pad 1 identifies the winding start.

The native RL fixture covers one 1 µH order code per series and checks routing, DRC, Gerber and BOM output, ideal scope data, and the breakout diagnostic.
