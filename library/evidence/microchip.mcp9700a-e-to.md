# MCP9700A-E/TO

See the [manufacturer datasheet and package drawing](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/APID/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/MCP970X-Data-Sheet-DS20001942.pdf). Pin 1 is VDD, pin 2 is VOUT, and pin 3 is GND. The nominal transfer has 10 mV/°C gain and 500 mV intercept. The staggered educational footprint derives from [Example 068](../../examples/068-esp32-telemetry-mock.adigun.json). Hardware fit, numerical device model, and human review remain open.
