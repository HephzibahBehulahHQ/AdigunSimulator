# KEMET C1206C C0G ordering evidence

[KEMET C1003_C0G](https://content.kemet.com/datasheets/kem_c1003_c0g_smd.pdf), dated 20 February 2025, lists all seven voltage columns for the selected 100–1,000 pF C1206C entries in Table 1A on pages 5 and 6.
