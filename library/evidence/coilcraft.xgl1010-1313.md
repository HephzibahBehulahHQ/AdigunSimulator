# Coilcraft XGL1010 and XGL1313 ordering evidence

The [XGL1010 series table](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/power/high-voltage-inductors/xgl/xgl1010/) lists 17 MED codes from 0.27 to 56 µH. The [XGL1313 series table](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/power/high-voltage-inductors/xgl/xgl1313/) lists 11 MED codes from 1.5 to 47 µH. The records use the nominal inductance and ±20% tolerance of those listed order codes. Lifecycle, native mapping, model validation and independent review remain pending.

## Symbol and PCB mapping

The [XGL1010 drawing](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/d836bde2-3b61-4037-a3a3-180bb07ed709/xgl1010d.gif?lang=en-US) specifies 2.38 × 8.50 mm lands on 6.65 mm centers and a 10 mm maximum height. The [XGL1313 drawing](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/1e95a404-a436-4fb4-911b-6d43e31d12dc/xgl1313d.gif?lang=en-US) specifies 2.98 × 11.20 mm lands on 8.60 mm centers and a 13 mm maximum height. Local pad 1 follows the winding-start stripe.

All 28 candidates have checksummed assets. The native fixtures exercise XGL1010-122MED and XGL1313-152MED and check PCB DRC, Gerber, BOM identity, ideal RL scope data and the breadboard breakout diagnostic. Physical breakout placement, manufacturer-specific models and independent review remain pending.
