# Coilcraft WB, WBC, and TTWB ordering evidence

- [WB](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/transformers/wideband-rf-transformers/smt/wb/). 22 order codes.
- [TTWB](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/transformers/wideband-rf-transformers/smt/ttwb/). 22 order codes.
- [WBC](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/transformers/wideband-rf-transformers/smt/wbc/). 12 order codes.

The WB manufacturer table lists PCL as through-hole and SMLD as surface mount. WBC and TTWB are listed as surface mount. The records use distinct catalog order codes. All 56 candidates have winding symbols and PCB layout assets. Model validation and independent review remain pending.

## WB winding and PCB assets

The manufacturer diagrams specify [WB10XX](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/474d1ed7-d4b5-4a30-9036-946950d09410/wbts1.gif) without taps, [WB20XX](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/896dd307-b0cb-4c17-8422-d5d7158e911d/wbts2.gif) with a secondary tap, and [WB30XX](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/83eafdd7-7a5f-4e4b-a343-500b68f7ddff/wbts3.gif) with taps on both windings. The [mechanical and PCB drawings](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/243268cf-0233-4ce2-a3a9-ae623722cbca/wbtd-%281%29.gif) identify omitted terminals and distinguish PCL drill geometry from SMLD rectangular lands. Through-hole copper annular rings and native RF analysis remain pending.

## WBC and TTWB winding and PCB assets

The [WBC winding diagrams](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/bf5fd63d-eb9f-4158-bd49-2949de9d620d/wbcs.gif) show four topologies. Variant D reverses the primary dot position. The [WBC land pattern](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/9c44bf18-532b-4b25-a1fd-68ec3c9b92f1/wbcd.gif) places pin 1 at the upper left.

TTWB has [untapped](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/13da8341-9cc8-4bc2-aee0-601a8a131e6a/ttwbs_a.gif), [secondary-tapped](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/9183dfd7-d73f-481a-9009-3be384d53405/ttwbs_b.gif), and [dual-tapped](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/0e054b38-5e1c-455a-85cf-edeb5cbf840a/ttwbs_c.gif) variants. The [TTWB land pattern](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/934d405f-4d90-4122-84b2-14411347de55/ttwbd.gif) places pin 1 at the lower left. All 34 assets preserve all six package lands, including lands with no illustrated winding connection. Native RF simulation and board-level checks remain pending.
