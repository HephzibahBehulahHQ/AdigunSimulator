# Coilcraft XGL1712 ordering evidence

The [manufacturer series page](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/power/shielded-inductors/molded-inductor/xgl/xgl1712/) and [short-form catalog](https://www.coilcraft.com/getmedia/d46871f9-0aed-4757-ae22-69277909da05/ShortFormCatalog.pdf) support 15 MED order-code candidates covering 1–82 µH with ±20% tolerance. Lifecycle evidence, native mapping, model validation and independent review remain pending.

## Symbol and PCB mapping

The [XGL1712 drawing](https://www.coilcraft.com/getattachment/b0a80709-fafd-4ebe-af90-b18a6cc0bc1f/xgl1712d.gif?lang=en-US) specifies 3.80 × 15.10 mm lands on 11.86 mm centers and a 12 mm maximum body height. Local pad 1 follows the winding-start stripe. All 15 candidates have checksummed symbols and footprints. The XGL1712-102MED native RL fixture checks winding-start placement, PCB DRC, Gerber, BOM identity, ideal scope output, and the breakout diagnostic.
